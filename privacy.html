Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026. Please have this reviewed by a qualified adviser before relying on it.

This policy explains how RésuméKlinik handles personal information on this website, in our CV and career services, and on Talents Klinik, our career platform at resumeklinik.com/talentsklinik. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

Who we are

RésuméKlinik is a career services business based in Lagos, Nigeria. We are the data controller for the information described here. Contact us about anything in this policy at email or on WhatsApp.

What we collect

  • Enquiries and services: your name, phone number, email, industry, the service you want, and any documents you choose to share, such as your CV.
  • Newsletter: your email address.
  • Talents Klinik members: your account email and password (stored only in encrypted form), and the profile you build: education, course, class of degree, NYSC status, qualifications, experience, target role, skills, industries and location. Also your CV text, CV review results, personality assessment answers and result, skill-gap analysis, learning plan, certificates you record, cover letters and LinkedIn drafts you create, and your matching preferences.
  • Employers: company name, website, contact name, work email, phone, industry, company size, the roles you hire for, roles you post, candidates you shortlist and introductions you request.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running.

Guest mode

If you use Talents Klinik without an account, your work is saved only in your own browser on your own device. We do not receive it, except the specific text you send for an AI feature (see below) at the moment you use it.

How we use it, and why we are allowed to

  • To reply to you and deliver services you ask for (performing our agreement with you).
  • To run your Talents Klinik account and its tools (performing our agreement with you).
  • To show your anonymised profile to verified employers and arrange introductions, only if you opt in to matching (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).
  • To send the newsletter (your consent; every email has an unsubscribe option).
  • To verify employers and protect candidates from misuse (our legitimate interest in a safe platform).

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

AI features

When you use a Talents Klinik AI tool (CV review, skill-gap analysis, role suggestions, cover letters, LinkedIn optimiser, personality summary or the career coach), the text needed for that request, such as your CV or your question together with your profile details, is sent to Anthropic, the provider of the Claude AI model, which generates the response. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms for API customers, which do not permit using that content to train its models. AI feedback is guidance only and can be wrong; please check it before relying on it.

Employer matching

Matching is off until you switch it on. If you opt in:

  • Employers we have verified and approved can see an anonymised version of your profile: education, skills, experience level, CV score, work-personality summary, number of certificates, availability and location preferences. They do not see your name, email or phone number.
  • If an employer asks to be introduced to you, Talents Klinik reviews the request. Only if we approve it do we share your contact details with that employer, and only for that opportunity.
  • You can leave the matching pool at any time from Get matched. Employers then stop seeing your profile straight away.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of providers to run the service. Each processes data only on our instructions:

  • Netlify: website hosting, our contact and newsletter forms, and the secure connection to the AI service.
  • Supabase: Talents Klinik accounts and the database that stores member and employer information.
  • Anthropic: the AI features, as described above.
  • Google Fonts: the typeface on our pages. Your browser requests it from Google, which sees your IP address.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) and email: only when you choose to contact us that way.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

International transfers

These providers may store or process data outside Nigeria, including in the European Union and the United States. Where this happens we rely on their contractual and security commitments, as the NDPA requires.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and CVs sent for a service: as long as needed to deliver the service and handle follow-up, and no longer than 24 months after our last contact.
  • Talents Klinik accounts: until you delete your data or your account. You can delete everything yourself under Account & data.
  • Employer accounts and introduction records: while the account is active, then up to 24 months for our records.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.

Security

Connections to the site are encrypted. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider in encrypted form, and we never see them. Database rules restrict each member to their own data, keep employers to anonymised profiles, and limit contact details to Talents Klinik administrators.

Your rights

Under the NDPA you may ask to access, correct or delete your information, object to or restrict how we use it, withdraw consent at any time, and receive a copy of your Talents Klinik data. Members can download or delete their data themselves under Account & data. For anything else, contact us and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Talents Klinik uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to save guest-mode work on your device. Nothing else is stored.

Age

Talents Klinik is intended for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, for members, tell you in the app.

The CV check-up

Answers to the free CV check-up on this website are processed in your browser and are not sent to us.
