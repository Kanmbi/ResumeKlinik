About the Founder

A Career Professional
Who Has Walked the Path

"I know exactly what recruiters look for — and what most CVs consistently fail to deliver."

Olusesi Mayowa Bastu, the founder of RésuméKlinik, brings over a decade of cross-sector professional experience spanning financial services, operations, business development, and talent management across Nigeria's most demanding and competitive environments.

Having personally navigated the rigorous hiring processes of top-tier institutions, Mayowa developed an insider understanding of what Nigerian and international recruiters actually look for in a candidate — and the critical narrative gaps that cause otherwise qualified professionals to be overlooked.

That deep knowledge is the foundation of every CV, LinkedIn profile, and career document produced by RésuméKlinik. The approach is clinical and precise: diagnose the gap between where a client is today and where they aspire to be, then prescribe the exact narrative and positioning strategy that closes it — with precision, evidence, and power.

Mayowa's philosophy goes beyond document writing. RésuméKlinik is a career intelligence clinic — a space where ambitious professionals get the expert diagnosis, strategic repositioning, and market intelligence they need to compete and win at the highest levels of industry.
