The journey

From the inside of hiring to the boardroom

Foundation

Economics, people and how they grow

A degree in Economics Education from the University of Lagos built a grounding in economics and in how people learn and develop. An MBA in Human Resource Management followed, deepening that focus on people and organisations.

Inside HR

Seeing how hiring really works

Human resources and talent roles within two global FMCG multinationals and a people advisory firm, with exposure across energy, fintech and financial services. This is where Mayowa saw how shortlists are made, and why strong candidates are so often missed.

2020

RésuméKlinik is founded

A career clinic built on a simple idea: diagnose before you prescribe. Since then, hundreds of professionals have been repositioned for roles in banking, consulting, technology, the public sector and international organisations.

2025

Management consulting at a global advisory firm

Advisory work across business process diagnostics, regulatory advisory, governance, monitoring and evaluation, and transaction advisory, including salary benchmarking and role scoping. Mayowa also serves as an ISO 27001 Information Security Champion.

2026

Senior Consultant, and building Talents Klinik

Promoted to Senior Consultant, while building Talents Klinik: an AI-powered career intelligence platform designed with, and for, Africa's next generation of talent.
