About RésuméKlinik

From a side practice to a career clinic

RésuméKlinik did not begin as a business plan. It began as one graduate helping others get seen, and grew alongside a career spent inside HR and consulting.

2018 · Oh Concepts

It starts with a simple problem

Shortly after university, Mayowa began helping fellow graduates and early-career professionals present themselves to employers: CVs, applications and how to describe experience so it counts. The practice was called Oh Concepts, and it had one aim: help capable people show their value in the labour market.

2019 – 2020 · The other side of the table

Learning how hiring really works

Mayowa's own career moved into human resources, through workforce administration, recruitment, HR business partnering, compensation and payroll inside global FMCG multinationals. Seeing shortlists made from the inside revealed the gap the business would come to focus on: the difference between having experience and being able to demonstrate why it makes you valuable to a specific employer.

2020 · RésuméKlinik

Oh Concepts becomes RésuméKlinik

The name changed because the idea had changed. A CV is rarely the whole problem; it is the symptom of a positioning problem. So RésuméKlinik was built as a clinic: diagnose where someone is, where they are going and what is stopping the market from seeing their value, then prescribe. Your career, diagnosed. Transformed.

2021 – 2022 · Depth

Sharper method, stronger foundation

An MBA in Human Resource Management, alongside more senior HR work, deepened the method. RésuméKlinik now understood both sides of the employment transaction: how candidates present themselves, and what employers are actually looking for.

2022 – 2025 · Consulting discipline

From rewriting CVs to diagnosing careers

Moving into HR consulting, then management consulting at a global advisory firm, brought structured problem solving, client advisory and the habit of starting from evidence. The service grew from CV writing into career positioning: LinkedIn profiles, cover letters, executive narratives and career strategy, each built from the same diagnosis.

2025 · Talents Klinik

From positioning to matching

Positioning solves one part of Nigeria's employment problem. The other is matching: graduates who do not know which path fits them, employers who cannot find the skills they need, and talent that cannot communicate what it has. Talents Klinik was created to close that gap, combining personality and skills assessment, learning plans and employer matching in one platform.

Today

A career clinic, headquartered in Lagos

More than 500 CVs transformed for professionals across Africa and the diaspora, in banking, consulting, technology, energy, the public sector and international organisations. RésuméKlinik now covers CV writing, LinkedIn optimisation, executive branding, cover letters and career consultancy, with Talents Klinik in early access.

The arc

Documents, positioning, intelligence, matching

Each stage of RésuméKlinik grew out of the last. Together they are one answer to one question: how does a capable person get seen by the right employer?

Documents

  • Achievement-led, ATS-ready CVs
  • Cover letters written for one employer, not every employer
  • Professional summaries and achievements addenda

Positioning

  • LinkedIn profiles that get the right messages
  • Executive and leadership narratives
  • Career transitions and international applications

Intelligence and matching

  • Personality and skills assessment
  • Skill gap analysis and learning plans
  • Employer matching through Talents Klinik
